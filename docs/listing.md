## Title

Idle World Closer

## Description

A small module that automatically signs out all idle players or even shuts down the game world directly when an idle Gamemaster is connected.

Players are warned before they are signed out or the world is shut down. Times are configurable via the module's settings.

Born out of my personal needs for my self-hosted Foundry server to shut down the game world when no one needs it.
